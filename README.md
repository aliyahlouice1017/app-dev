# Wednesday

## About the Series
**Wednesday** is a *supernatural mystery comedy* series on **Netflix**. It follows Wednesday Addams as a student at Nevermore Academy, a school for outcasts. The first season premiered in November 2022.

## Main Characters
- Wednesday Addams (played by Jenna Ortega)
- Enid Sinclair (played by Emma Myers)
- Morticia Addams (played by Catherine Zeta-Jones)
- Gomez Addams (played by Luis Guzmán)
- Thing

## Why I Love It
1. The dark humor
2. The mystery and plot twists
3. Wednesday's style and personality
4. The Nevermore Academy setting

## Favorite Quote
> "I'm not a fan of hugs."

## Fun Facts
- Directed in part by **Tim Burton**
- Based on the characters from *The Addams Family*
- Genre: `Mystery / Comedy / Fantasy`
